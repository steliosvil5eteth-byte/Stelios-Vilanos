# Ελλάδα VS Γερμανία — πηγές, ορισμοί και φωτογραφίες

Εκδοτική σύγκριση για15/10/2026. Κάθε ζεύγος χωρών αφορά τον ίδιο δείκτη και έτος. Δεν είναι ενιαίος βαθμός ευημερίας.

## Πρωτογενή δεδομένα

### salary

2024 annual gross average full-time-adjusted salary per employee, EUR. Greece 17954; Germany 53791. Direct Eurostat API values saved as salary_EL.json and salary_DE.json; not net wages or a job offer.

https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/en/web/products-eurostat-news/w/ddn-20251112-1



### hours

2024 actual weekly hours in main job, employed persons aged 20-64, full-time and part-time together. Greece39.8/Germany33.9; not contractual full-time hours or commuting.

https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/de/web/products-eurostat-news/w/ddn-20250514-1



### prices

2025 preliminary household final consumption price-level indices, EU27=100: Greece87.4/Germany108.3. Statistics Norway, source Eurostat, updated19June2026. Not inflation or purchasing power.

https://www.ssb.no/en/priser-og-prisindekser/konsumpriser/statistikk/sammenlikning-av-prisniva-i-europa



### housing

2024 housing costs as share of disposable household income. Rounded values Greece36%,Germany25%; not the percentage of households exceeding a40%cost threshold.

https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/fr/web/interactive-publications/housing-2025



### education

PISA2025, share of15-year-old students reaching at least Level2 in mathematics: Greece50%,Germany66%. Both current OECD country notes verified15Oct2026. Not a comprehensive education ranking.

https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/pisa-2025-results-volume-i-country-notes_2d4ff9ea-en/greece_dd7e428b-en.html

https://www.oecd.org/de/publications/pisa-2025-ergebnisse-band-i_eade94cd-de/deutschland_8a1ce578-de.html

### health

2023 life expectancy at birth, both sexes, OECD Health at a Glance2025 dashboard: Greece81.8,Germany81.1years. Not hospital-quality ranking.

https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/health-at-a-glance-2025_8f9e3f98-en/full-report/indicator-overview-country-dashboards-and-major-trends_368ac397.html



### roads

2024 road fatalities per million residents: Greece64,Germany33. Population denominator, not distance driven or total infrastructure quality.

https://transport.ec.europa.eu/news-events/news/road-safety-statistics-2024-progress-continues-amid-persistent-challenges-2025-10-17_en



### digital_greece

SpecialEurobarometer2025:78%ofGreek citizens consider digitisation of public and private services makes life easier. Opinion survey, not objective service-quality score.

https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/factpages/greece-2025-digital-decade-country-report



### digital_germany

Same2025Eurobarometer item:74%ofGerman citizens. Do not interpret the4point difference as statistical significance or an objective country ranking.

https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/factpages/germany-2025-digital-decade-country-report
