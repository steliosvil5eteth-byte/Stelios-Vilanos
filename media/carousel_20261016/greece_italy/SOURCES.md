# Ελλάδα VS Ιταλία — πηγές και άδειες

Για τη δημοσίευση της 16ης Οκτωβρίου 2026. Κάθε ζεύγος χωρών συγκρίνεται με τον ίδιο δείκτη και έτος. Τα διαφορετικά έτη 2022–2025 αναγράφονται καθαρά. Δεν πρόκειται για κατάταξη ευημερίας, για εξατομικευμένη οικονομική συμβουλή ή για ισχυρισμό των πιο πρόσφατων τιμών.

## Πρωτογενή δεδομένα

### salary

2024 annual gross average full-time-adjusted salary per employee, EUR. Greece17954,Italy33523. Direct Eurostat API responses retained in italy_sources. Not net monthly pay, median salary or a job offer.

https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/en/web/products-eurostat-news/w/ddn-20251112-1

### hours

2024 actual weekly working hours in main job, employed persons aged20-64, all NACE activities, both sexes, full-time and part-time together. Greece39.8,Italy36.1. Not contracted full-time hours or commuting time.

https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/de/web/products-eurostat-news/w/ddn-20250514-1

### prices

2024 household final consumption expenditure price-level index,EU27=100. Greece86.5,Italy97.0. Preliminary Eurostat PPP snapshot17Dec2025 displayed by NSI. Not inflation and not a specific household budget. Not claimed to be the latest2026price levels.

https://www.nsi.bg/index.php/en/statistical-data/109/360

### housing

2024 share of housing costs in disposable household income; hhcompTOTAL,rskpovthTOTAL,unitPC. Greece35.5%,Italy13.6%. Exact direct-API values. Not average rent, only renters, or percentage of households exceeding40%costs.

https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/ilc_mded01/default/table?lang=en

### education

OECD PISA2022 mathematics mean score among15-year-old students: Greece430,Italy471, rounded to whole points. Country snapshot tables. Not an overall ranking of schools, education systems or universities.

https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/pisa-2022-results-volume-i_53f23881-en/full-report/overview-of-performance-trends_ae540546.html

### health

Eurostat2023 life expectancy at birth, both sexes: Greece81.6years,Italy83.8years. The first table column is explicitly2023. Multiple determinants; not a score of hospital quality.

https://knowledge4policy.ec.europa.eu/health-promotion-knowledge-gateway/health-inequalities-rates-1_en

### roads

Final2024 road deaths per million residents: Greece64,Italy51. Denominator is population, not distance driven. Does not rank all infrastructure.

https://transport.ec.europa.eu/news-events/news/road-safety-statistics-2024-progress-continues-amid-persistent-challenges-2025-10-17_en

### digital_greece

Special Eurobarometer Digital Decade2025:78%of Greek citizens say digitalisation of public and private services makes life easier. Opinion survey, not an objective service-quality score.

https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/factpages/greece-2025-digital-decade-country-report

### digital_italy

Same2025Eurobarometer item:73%of Italian citizens. No claim of statistical significance or objective country ranking.

https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/factpages/italy-2025-digital-decade-country-report
