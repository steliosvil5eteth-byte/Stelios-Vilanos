Privacy Notice — Draft

Version: 2026-09-20-v1

Pre-launch draft. Controller identity, contact details, legal bases, processors, international-transfer details and final retention periods must be completed and reviewed before commercial launch.

Data handled by the application

The application can store account identifiers, email, subscription plan, research settings, watchlists, paper trades, strategy history, audit events, consent records and hashed security-event network identifiers. Broker passwords are not stored by the application.

Purposes

Data is used to authenticate users, provide the research/paper-trading service, enforce security and plan limits, synchronize user settings, generate reports and investigate abuse or operational failures.

Security and retention

Passwords created in the application are stored using salted scrypt hashes. Sessions are revocable, authenticated writes use CSRF protection, sensitive endpoints are rate limited, and audit/security histories are bounded. Production deployments should use a persistent database and encrypted secrets.

User controls

The application includes self-service export and authenticated deletion of server account data. Legal retention exceptions and verified identity procedures must be finalized before public launch.
