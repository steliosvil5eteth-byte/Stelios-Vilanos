Purpose

Trading Control Center is designed for market research, backtesting and paper-trading workflows. It does not provide individualized investment advice and the current product has no live-trading endpoint.

User responsibility

Users are responsible for their own investment decisions, account security, configuration choices and review of any third-party broker or market-data terms. Scores, backtests and paper results are analytical outputs, not guarantees or promises of future performance.

Availability and data

Market data, APIs and broker services may be delayed, incomplete, unavailable or incorrect. Features can be limited by subscription plan, third-party service limits or safety controls.

Accounts

Users must protect credentials and must not attempt to bypass plan limits, security controls, paper-only restrictions or broker safeguards.

Subscriptions

Payment processing is not active in this version. Before billing is enabled, final pricing, renewal, cancellation, refund and tax terms must be inserted here and reviewed.

Termination

Accounts may be suspended for abuse, security risks or material violations of these terms. Users may request or use available self-service export/deletion functions subject to security verification.

Liability and governing law

Final limitation-of-liability, dispute, governing-law and business-identity clauses are intentionally left for legal review before launch.
