Paper Trading & Algorithmic Testing Risk Disclosure

Version: 2026-09-20-v1

Paper trading and backtesting can materially overstate results that would have occurred with real money.

Key risks

  • Backtests can suffer from overfitting, look-ahead bias, survivorship bias and insufficient sample size.
  • Paper fills may differ from real fills because of spreads, liquidity, slippage, latency and partial executions.
  • Market-data feeds and broker APIs can be delayed, incomplete or unavailable.
  • A strategy that performed well historically can stop working as market conditions change.
  • Correlation between positions can make portfolio risk much larger than the number of positions suggests.
  • Stops and targets reduce risk but do not guarantee an execution price.

The product is intended to support research and testing. Users remain responsible for reviewing outputs and making their own decisions.
