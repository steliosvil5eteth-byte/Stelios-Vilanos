Terms of Use — Pre-launch Draft

Version: 2026-09-25-signal-first-v1

Pre-launch draft. Qualified legal/regulatory review is required before commercial distribution.

Purpose

Trading Signal Center is a market-research and signal-analysis product. It can analyze market bars, event/news data and user-imported read-only holdings. The current release has no live-order endpoint and does not custody customer assets.

Signals

Signals may display direction, model score, entry reference, stop reference, target reference and supporting factors. Model scores are not probabilities and outputs are not guarantees or promises of future performance.

User responsibility

The user independently decides whether to execute a transaction and is responsible for broker/exchange choice, order entry, account security, suitability, taxes, costs and risk.

Portfolio data

Manual/read-only holdings are used to display concentration and existing-exposure context. No broker password, withdrawal permission or live trading credential is required by this release.

Availability and third-party data

Market data, news, filings and third-party APIs can be delayed, unavailable or inaccurate. Commercial use may require separate data-provider rights or licenses.

Plans

Current product tiers are Demo, Starter and Pro. Payment processing remains disabled or test-only until commercial launch requirements, taxes, cancellation/refund terms and regulatory review are complete.

Regulatory perimeter

Features that would constitute regulated individualized investment advice, discretionary management, order execution or custody must not be enabled for commercial users unless the required legal/regulatory structure is in place.

Liability and governing law

Final business identity, governing law, consumer terms, liability and dispute clauses remain subject to legal review before launch.
