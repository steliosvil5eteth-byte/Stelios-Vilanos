Signal, Market-Data & Research Risk Disclosure

Version: 2026-09-25-signal-first-v1

A model score such as 95/100 is a ranking/confidence score produced by the system. It is not a 95% probability of profit and is not a guarantee of performance.

Key risks

  • Signals can be wrong. Markets can move immediately against a signal.
  • The 5% model stop is a reference level, not a guaranteed execution price. Gaps, slippage, liquidity, latency and exchange conditions can produce larger losses.
  • The 5–10% target is a model objective, not a promised return.
  • News, filings and market-data feeds can be delayed, incomplete, duplicated or incorrect.
  • Backtests and forward tests can overstate live results and may suffer from regime change, sample-size limitations and model overfitting.
  • Imported holdings are read-only research context. The application does not hold customer assets or broker credentials and does not place live orders in this release.
  • Portfolio concentration can increase losses even when individual positions use stops.

Users remain responsible for deciding whether to act on any output and for reviewing their broker/exchange execution, costs, taxes and risks.
