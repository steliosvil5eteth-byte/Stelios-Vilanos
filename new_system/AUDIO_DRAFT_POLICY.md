# Audio drafts — safe offline policy

Use only local, no-cost Greek speech synthesis for rough timing previews. The installed eSpeak Greek voice is synthetic and does not meet final human-quality narration standards. Render each scene separately, measure actual WAV duration with ffprobe, and export per-scene timestamps and one concatenated audio draft per story. Do not call paid TTS, consume HeyGen credits, publish, or mark production-ready. Final voice selection and audible QA remain required before release.
